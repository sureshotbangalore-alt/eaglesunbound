# Solis dealer ecosystem executive brief

Prepared for Raja and company leadership. Research cutoff 3 November 2026.

**Decision requested:** approve bounded discovery and a measured Dutch dealer pilot before committing to a new platform. Prioritise reuse, correct-part identification and reliable dealer handoffs.

The Netherlands already has a guided selector, a verified S26/H26 comparison table, S26 configurator, price list, dealer information, quote/demo enquiry and public warranty-registration form. AgriXpert also has tractor listings with prices, options and cart entry. These capabilities change the investment question from building basic discovery to connecting dealer work and validating operational gaps. [Dutch S26](https://www.solistractor.nl/modellen/s26/), [AgriXpert example](https://www.agrixpert.com/solis-16-4wd).

**Fresh Dutch priorities.** The demonstration page contains unfinished instructions about follow-up. A narrow-opening selector result includes records showing zero widths. Registration is public, but its no-plate intake and extension eligibility need a clear distinction. Correct or validate these points with the accountable owners before widening recommendations or automating promises. [Demonstration route](https://www.solistractor.nl/demonstratie/?model=s26), [selector result](https://www.solistractor.nl/keuzehulp/?situation=laden&transmission=shuttle&width=100), [registration](https://www.solistractor.nl/garantieformulier/).

The published roster is 19 dealers and one subdealer, rather than 20 independent wholesale dealer contracts. Generic stock labels are not per-model availability. The proposal must preserve main-dealer/subdealer relationships and obtain authoritative supply data before making commitments. [Dutch network](https://www.solistractor.nl/dealers/).

A blanket claim that Solis has no dealer portal is unsupported. Germany exposes a dealer login, Romania links DMS/IBIZ and Solis USA describes dealer-portal parts ordering. Dutch eligibility, functions and integration rights remain unverified. [German login](https://www.solis-traktor.de/haendlerlogin), [US service](https://solistractorusa.com/content/sportsman/service.php).

**Identity must be resolved.** “Solis Structures NL” is not a verified legal company. ITL's Solis Tractors & Agricultural Machinery B.V. and AgriXpert/DIGO must remain distinct. ICRA reports the B.V. as wholly ITL owned from FY2025 accounts; AgriXpert describes its Dutch importer role and DIGO membership. Current registry extracts and dealer contracts are still needed. [ICRA](https://www.icra.in/Rating/GetRationalReportFilePdf?id=139360), [AgriXpert](https://www.agrixpert.com/).

**European scope correction:** solis.eu redirects to an unrelated appliance business. Tractor European research uses Solis World's EU range and official country routes. [Solis World](https://solisworld.com/), [namesake checked](https://solis.eu/).

The four workstreams should share identity, machine/product records and approvals:

- **Dealer Portal:** common task queue and preserved enquiry context, linked to existing systems.
- **Parts Intelligence:** machine/engine identity, licensed diagrams and verified fitment before supply commitments.
- **Agentic Commerce:** source-linked answers and staff-reviewed drafts; consequential actions require authorised approval and acknowledgement.
- **Dealer Onboarding:** qualification, agreements, training and role provisioning measured through first-order readiness.

**Financial evidence supports investigation, not a Solis ROI.** No reliable Solis machine/parts split was established. AGCO's audited 2025 replacement parts were 18.58% of group sales. Titan Machinery's parts plus service contributed 24.98% of FY2026 revenue and 63.25% of gross profit. OEM and dealer economics have different scopes and cannot be applied to Solis. [AGCO annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/880266/000088026626000027/agco2025annualreport_ars.htm), [Titan 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1409171/000162828026022376/titn-20260131.htm).

The financial workbook separates sourced actuals from illustrative low/base/high scenarios and leaves actual Solis inputs blank. Illustration per 1,000 eligible annual requests produces 25/100/200 capacity hours and EUR995/3,980/7,960 gross opportunity equivalents before cost. These are assumed examples, include non-cash capacity and are not Solis forecasts, cash savings or ROI.

**Release gates:** confirm the legal sponsor and data rights; inspect existing private systems; observe dealer tasks; obtain a licensed serial-to-part sample; establish local baselines and benefit ownership; demonstrate reliable approvals and integrations; then decide rollout scope. Assess another importer only after the Dutch operating model is proven.

No direct interviews, private-system access, transaction tests or certified registry extracts were obtained. The report labels these gaps and supplies specific stakeholder requests. Public contact routes do not establish that back-office tasks are manual.
