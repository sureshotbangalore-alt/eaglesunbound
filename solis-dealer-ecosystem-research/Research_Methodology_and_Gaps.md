# Research methodology and remaining gaps

Research cutoff and access date: 3 November 2026, Europe Amsterdam. Prepared for Raja and company leadership.

## Work completed

The workspace was inspected for earlier Solis documents; none was located among available report files. Fresh public research covered Solis World, its EU range and country links, the Netherlands importer/dealer sites, selected German and Romanian routes, Solis USA as a reuse clue, official competitor documentation, industry association material, qualitative forums and original financial disclosures. Independent areas were researched in parallel as requested in the assignment.

Actual public routes included Dutch selector choices, S26 configuration/options and quote form, the effective-dated PDF price list, AgriXpert product/category pages, dealer/service/warranty routes, regional login entry points and public dealer application forms. Forms were inspected without submission. No purchases, accounts, messages to third parties or warranty claims were initiated.

The refreshed evidence register contains 92 entries representing 71 distinct original URLs. Duplicate sources retain their research IDs and are grouped by original URL in the report appendix. Verified means direct support from a reviewed source, including explicitly attributed company descriptions. Reported, Inference, Hypothesis and Open question preserve weaker evidence or analytical status. Dates absent from originals are recorded as not stated. A page's upload path is not treated as its effective date.

Material financial figures were taken from ICRA's reproduction of ITL indicators, AGCO's audited annual report, Titan's audited 10-K and reviewed CNH/Deere/Kubota disclosures. Calculations retain reporting entity, year, scope, unit and denominator. No peer percentage is applied to Solis. The workbook contains an independent scenario build, not a Solis forecast.

## Dutch website refresh

The follow-up specifically re-opened solistractor.nl and inspected selection, a populated comparison, configuration and quote forms, demonstration intake, dealer/subdealer directory, parts/service routing, warranty terms, Dutch registration, privacy and the related importer shop. For four material findings, an independent direct public HTTPS retrieval confirmed HTTP 200 and the relevant returned content. No form, lookup, upload, payment or private workflow was executed. The computer-use helper could not establish the browser URL confidently, so native interaction/layout validation did not proceed. These limits do not negate the public page evidence.

Material corrections are explicit in the main report: comparison and Dutch registration are publicly present; machine history and registration processing remain unverified. The demo page's unfinished follow-up copy is an observed content issue. Selector zero widths and cross-channel S16 specifications require product-owner reconciliation. Optional-plate intake versus extension policy may concern different stages and must not be turned into an invented rejection rule. Source access dates do not establish feature deployment dates.

The fresh source records preserve original URLs and distinguish public presence from successful execution. Earlier source limitations superseded by the refresh are identified. Duplicate originals retain research IDs for traceability and are grouped by URL in the report appendix.

## Access and evidence limits

- No authenticated Solis or competitor portal was entered. Germany's login and Romania's DMS/IBIZ links establish public entry points only. A company description establishes the US portal claim, not Dutch access.
- The actual Dutch S26/H26 comparison was retrieved successfully after earlier tool timeouts. The Dutch warranty-registration form was also verified, correcting incomplete earlier coverage. The narrow-opening selector returned records with zero widths; browser layout, all other branches and actual engineering widths were not tested. Some global pages timed out and a document centre required Google sign-in.
- A Solis USA brochure URL returned 404. Indexed text was not promoted into verified evidence.
- Deere originals sometimes returned JavaScript-only bodies or access errors; technical API claims were not treated as verified. Its JD early-access announcement is retained as limited-access Reported evidence.
- Some CNH PDF/course leads were inaccessible. The opened 2016 sustainability report is expressly a historical precedent, not a current process assertion.
- Latest NAEDA proprietary survey results were protected. Only the public survey scope was used; no scores or Dutch/Solis findings were inferred.
- Forum statements have unverified identities and mixed local experiences. They are qualitative signals and do not establish prevalence.
- Full ITL audited statements and FY2026 audited indicators were not obtained. ICRA evidence is classified Reported. A consolidation-scope ambiguity remains between its finance exclusion language and annexure.
- No certified current Dutch registry extract, dealer contract, data-processing agreement, catalogue licence, ERP inventory or real serial/build sample was supplied.
- No actual Solis revenue split, contribution margins, installed base, task time, lead conversion, return rate, adoption, implementation cost or payback was established.

8. Mitsubishi/Yanmar relationships do not establish fitment or catalogue reuse rights for every tractor.
9. Parts/service peer financial definitions remain separate from Solis and from one another.

## Required validation before investment

Proposed sample sizes are operational starting points, not representative survey claims.

The illustrative model assumes one eligible parts order per identification request, separates non-labour return cost from productivity time and treats capacity equivalent separately from cash savings. Replace these assumptions with observed denominators and attributable effects.
