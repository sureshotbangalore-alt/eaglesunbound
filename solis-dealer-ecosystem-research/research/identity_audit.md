# Fresh corporate identity and public digital audit

Research access date: 3 November 2026. Scope: global brand, Netherlands importer and selected European country journeys. Sources are opened originals unless expressly marked inaccessible. No forms submitted, accounts created, purchases initiated or private logins accessed. Evidence class Verified means the public page directly supports the statement; it does not independently authenticate marketing claims.

## Executive findings

1. **The phrase Solis Structures NL remains unresolved.** Searches for that exact phrase did not establish a tractor business with that legal name. Treat it as Raja's working reference, not a contracting party. Request the exact legal name, KvK extract and project sponsor before contracting.
2. **ITL and AgriXpert are distinct organisations.** ITL manufactures Solis tractors and describes Solis as its brand. ICRA's 28 November 2025 rating report, Annexure II page 6, lists Solis Tractors & Agricultural Machinery B.V. as 100% owned and fully consolidated, citing ITL's FY2025 consolidated annual report. This is strong historical corporate evidence; current ownership should be confirmed with a fresh filing. AgriXpert describes itself as part of DIGO and as the Dutch importer/support organisation; no source reviewed establishes ITL ownership of AgriXpert. [I02](https://solisworld.com/become-a-dealer/) [I27](https://www.icra.in/Rating/GetRationalReportFilePdf?id=139360) [I04](https://www.solistractor.nl/dealers/agrixpert/) [I05](https://www.agrixpert.com/)
3. **solis.eu is a namesake, not a tractor source.** Opening that domain redirects to solis.de, a household appliance site describing a Swiss family business with coffee, kitchen, beauty and climate products. Exclude its checkout, parts and service capabilities from this tractor analysis. Tractor Europe is represented by the EU range on solisworld.com and separate country sites linked by its selector. [I01](https://solis.eu/) [I06](https://solisworld.com/)
4. **Existing Netherlands capabilities materially reduce the case for rebuilding discovery.** There is a real three-question selector, a detailed S26 configurator, an indicative price list and dealer-selected quotation form. AgriXpert also provides tractor ecommerce with price, options and a visible stock label. Improve data consistency and the dealer handoff before commissioning another selector or storefront. This recommendation is an inference, not an established IT roadmap. [I07](https://www.solistractor.nl/keuzehulp/) [I08](https://www.solistractor.nl/modellen/s26/) [I09](https://www.agrixpert.com/solis-16-4wd)
5. **A dealer portal cannot be described as absent.** Germany has an explicit dealer login on an ILAFA-operated site. Romania's official-country-linked site exposes DMS/IBIZ links to crm.sonalika.com/EMS/. Their authenticated functions and Netherlands eligibility remain unknown. [I10](https://www.solis-traktor.de/haendlerlogin) [I11](https://tractoarelesolis.ro/) [I12](https://www.solis-traktor.de/impressum)

## Actual journeys followed

### Netherlands tractor discovery and handoff

Opened the dealer page, followed the selector and selected **loading and moving**, then **frequent forward and reverse**, then **no known narrow opening**. The selector progressed from 12 to 8 to 4 candidates and produced a final result page. It displayed four matches in its heading, but the parsed result exposed three cards; two cards showed width as 0 cm. This is a concrete public data-quality flag, not proof the entire browser interface fails. A browser acceptance test should verify card count and suppress unknown dimensions. [I07](https://www.solistractor.nl/keuzehulp/) [I17](https://www.solistractor.nl/keuzehulp/?situation=laden&transmission=shuttle&width=any)

Opened S26 model detail: price from EUR 10,150 excluding VAT, tyre selection, loader options, hydraulics, front lift/PTO dependency and indicative configuration pricing. The page says prices derive from the 1 February 2026 list and requires dealer confirmation of execution, installation and availability. It offers postcode, contact fields, preferred dealer and work requirements in the quote form. **The presence of the form and choice of dealer are verified; delivery, CRM creation, saved configuration transfer and routing service levels were not tested.** [I08](https://www.solistractor.nl/modellen/s26/)

Opened the information page and followed its current price-list link. The 11-page PDF internally states **1 February 2026**, despite the August upload path. It includes model, tyre, implement, mounting and delivery options. Older price lists remain searchable; the internal effective date should govern. [I18](https://www.solistractor.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Solis-prijslijst.pdf)

### Importer ecommerce

Followed AgriXpert homepage to Solis category and Solis 16 product. The product shows EUR 6,950 excluding VAT / EUR 8,409.50 including VAT, an **in stock** label, tyres, loader and accessories. Cart entry points are present; the surrounding account interface advertises order/shipping status and order history. ### Parts and service

The Dutch service page asks owners to contact their regional dealer, and to have model, serial number and operating hours ready for technical issues. Parts compatibility is checked by the dealer; maintenance is arranged directly. This demonstrates a human-assisted public route, not proof dealers internally use paper/email or lack digital support. [I20](https://www.solistractor.nl/klantenservice/)

The Dutch information page says its operational parts route runs via Almere, names Mitsubishi compact engines and ITL larger engines, and mentions component suppliers. The global parts page instead describes Germany/Yanmar and 24–48-hour delivery. Treat the latter as a company marketing claim without an audited fulfilment SLA; ask which warehouse serves each NL SKU, who holds stock and which system provides available-to-promise data. The existence of Germany and Almere references may reflect different network layers, dates or markets; do not claim a contradiction has been resolved. [I21](https://www.solistractor.nl/informatie/) [I22](https://solisworld.com/tractor-parts/)

The Netherlands **become a dealer** link lands on a general contact form with dealer subject. No public qualification dashboard, credit checks, contracts, certification or provisioning automation verified. German dealer login is a separate observed surface. Romanian DMS/IBIZ link leads to an endpoint that timed out; its authenticated availability and features remain unverified. Its stated coverage differs from Netherlands public material; never carry one country's warranty terms into another market. ## Proposed ecosystem map specification

Public customer → Solisworld brand and EU/global catalogue → country selector → Solis Nederland product selector/configurator → dealer-selected enquiry → Dutch dealer advice, demonstration, final quotation and service. A parallel public purchase branch runs through AgriXpert ecommerce. AgriXpert is both importer/support organisation and listed local dealer. ITL supplies manufacturing/product authority. Parts distribution has published Germany/Yanmar and Netherlands Almere references, with operational integration unresolved. Separate Germany dealer login and Romania DMS entry demonstrate potential reuse dependencies. Unknown boundaries: CRM/lead routing, ERP, dealer DMS, serial/BOM records, warranty system, wholesale pricing, catalogue licensing, stock feeds and customer-data agreements.

## Recommended direct requests

- Sponsor: identify exact legal beneficiary and budget owner; supply current KvK extracts and group chart for Solis B.V. and AgriXpert.
- ITL: walk through existing DMS/IBIZ, list markets/roles, APIs, catalogue, warranty and machine-registration services; confirm Solis trademark and data licensing rights.
- Importer: provide redacted dealer contract, territory list, systems inventory and integration contacts; confirm Almere/Germany responsibilities and available-to-promise semantics.
- Website owners: show configurator rules, data source, pricing approval, lead payload, consent record, routing logs and handoff into dealer systems.
- Parts manager: demonstrate 10 real serial-to-part cases including engine variation, supersession, incorrect part, return and warranty; request authorised catalogue export and permitted uses.
- Dealers: observe actual quoting, parts and warranty tasks before claiming manual duplication; measure time, re-entry and failure causes.

## Access limitations and quality corrections

Repeated timeouts affected Dutch catalogue index, comparison and global maintenance-kit retrieval. Global download centre redirected to Google sign-in. DMS endpoint timed out. No private dealer credentials were supplied. Public pages can change and retrieval can expose older indexed variants; old solistractor.nl commerce paths coexist in search with newer pages. Cite current exact paths and dates, verify configuration data before automation, and confirm operational systems directly. No prior Solis report was supplied to this subtask; all findings above came from new source exploration.
