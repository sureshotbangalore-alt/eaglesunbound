# How to read the financial workbook

**2023 is the reporting year. The value 8,711 is money, not a tractor count.** It means AGCO earned USD 8,711 million in tractor sales revenue during 2023. This equals USD 8.711 billion, or USD 8,711,000,000. It is an AGCO group figure, not a Solis figure.

- **Start here** explains currencies, scales, financial terms and the distinction between published evidence and the hypothetical example.
- **Company financials** presents sales revenue and profit with the currency and scale visible in the cells. AGCO and Titan Machinery are comparison companies. ITL figures cover the company, not Solis separately.
- **Example only** uses Low, Base and High choices. The 1,000 input is parts requests per year, not tractors sold. Time valued in euros is not automatically a cash saving. The example is not observed Solis performance or a return-on-investment forecast.
- **Source data** retains all 43 original financial records and shows both the reported scale and the full currency amount. Original source links appear below the records.

USD means US dollars. INR means Indian rupees. JPY means Japanese yen. One million is 1,000,000. One crore is 10,000,000 Indian rupees. No exchange conversion has been made.

Revenue means money from sales before subtracting costs. Gross profit subtracts direct sales costs, before operating expenses. PAT means profit after tax. ICRA's label operating income means the revenue measure in the ITL source; it does not mean operating profit.

No verified Solis machine-versus-parts revenue split was established. Peer-company parts percentages must not be treated as Solis percentages. Actual Solis input fields remain blank until measured data is supplied.
