# Page consolidation - 5 November 2026

The public site now has 13 canonical pages:

Home, Bootcamp, Programmes, Activities, About, Founder, Gallery, Stay & Dining, Contact (including FAQs), Book Now, Privacy, Terms, Booking Policy.

Merged content:

- 70 activity detail pages -> searchable expandable Activities catalogue. Descriptions, requirements and activity enquiry actions are retained.
- 9 programme detail pages -> expandable Programmes catalogue. Descriptions, highlights, audience, activities and booking actions are retained.
- Corporate, Schools & Colleges, Kids Experiences -> audience guides under Programmes, retaining the published CMS sections.
- Accommodation -> Stay & Dining, retaining the published camping and room information alongside the programme menus.
- FAQs -> Contact.
- Mission selector and the duplicate Explore browser -> Programmes / Activities.
- Empty Packages, Clients and Testimonials -> Programmes / About.

Existing URLs redirect to the merged destination, including record-specific anchors. The sitemap and header/footer navigation list the consolidated destinations. CMS records remain available to administrators; consolidation removes separate public pages without deleting business information.

Standalone record pages now contain only fallback redirects. No deployment was performed.
