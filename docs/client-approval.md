# Information required before public launch

- Authentic campus/activity photographs, image descriptions, publication rights, approved video, a compressed delivery file and poster.
- Bootcamp format, duration, inclusions, exclusions, current availability, participant suitability, accessibility, clothing and equipment guidance.
- Any separate group/family programme, actual packages and approved prices, if the business wants them published.
- Updated team and leadership biographies, photographs and permission to publish names, ranks, qualifications and awards.
- Written approval for client names, logos, testimonials and case studies.
- Confirmation of phone numbers and address; verified WhatsApp Business number; public email; social profiles; Google Business Profile, location pin and opening hours.
- Final privacy policy, controller/data-contact details, retention/deletion periods, website terms, cancellation/refund conditions and approved waivers.
- Hosting/domain ownership, HTTPS configuration, PostgreSQL, object-storage credentials, backup ownership, notification delivery and operational monitoring.
- A complete legacy URL export and redirect mapping before changing the live domain.

The local website has no payment gateway, fabricated ratings, official military-affiliation claims, seeded customer records or unapproved certifications. Prelaunch legal text is explicitly labelled; the client must approve it before search indexing or public deployment.

The seed FAQ answers describe the implemented booking workflow and high-level business information. They remain editable and should be reviewed with the rest of the copy. No promised safety guarantees, age limits or inclusion policies are seeded.
