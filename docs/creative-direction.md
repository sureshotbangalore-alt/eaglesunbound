# Creative direction

Continue in the existing repository and preserve the FastAPI/PostgreSQL contract. The initial workspace was empty, so there were no previous application features to migrate or remove.

The visual direction is an outdoor editorial journal: oversized condensed Barlow headings, readable DM Sans body text, warm paper, charcoal and controlled red. Fonts are hosted locally. The supplied official logo is copied byte-for-byte, without redraw or filtering.

The page sequence moves from an immersive, video-ready opening to an editorial introduction, the bootcamp concept, staggered programme imagery, a typographic activity library and an invitation to enquire. Programme and activity detail pages prioritise useful descriptions and specific next steps. Contact and booking pages use a quieter, task-focused two-column composition. Mobile rearranges these sections and supplies a persistent call/booking bar.

Motion is limited to in-view reveals, media scale, navigation and button feedback. Native scroll is retained. Reduced-motion preference suppresses playback and visual movement; background video has a pause control and pauses offscreen. No GSAP dependency is necessary for these sequences.

Approval is part of the content model: unconfirmed packages, client logos, biographies and schedules are not replaced by invented proof. Empty client/gallery pages explain the preparation status and offer a useful next step. Stock imagery is labelled and remains a prelaunch limitation, not evidence of the client's location or facilities.
